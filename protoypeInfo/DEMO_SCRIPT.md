# Demo script (2–3 minutes)

This is the path I'll click through in the interview. The prototype must support it end to end, starting from **Reset demo**.

## 1. Set the scene (S0, 10 seconds)

"Namak Street sells on five channels. Every Monday the founder asks one question, and today it takes the analyst a day to answer, with numbers that don't match. Here's the same Monday with the Monday Number Engine."

Click **Start as the analyst (9:00)**.

## 2. Analyst: what's ready? (A1)

Point out:
- Shopify and Amazon arrived automatically through Hevo connectors.
- Zepto's upload is validated.
- Blinkit needs an upload. Instamart's report is missing.
- "3 of 5 channels ready", with confidence shown as reasons, not a score.

## 3. Analyst: upload Blinkit (A2)

Click **Upload report** → **Use sample file**.
- Validation catches a **missing Thursday** and an unexpected column.
- Click **Upload corrected file** → validated, 7 of 7 days.

Line to say: "The upload isn't just accepted; it's checked."

## 4. Analyst: map items (A3) — Moment 3

- "₹38,000 of Blinkit sales don't map to any Namak Street product yet."
- Click **Accept all suggestions**. Unmapped drops to ₹2,000.
- The Festive Pack has no SKU. Click **Leave unmapped**.

Line to say: "What we can't standardise, we show. That ₹2,000 will appear in the founder's report, not disappear."

## 5. Analyst: one gap check (A4) — Moment 4

Open **Shopify → View details**.
- Shopify's own dashboard says ₹4,18,000; we say ₹4,60,000.
- Refunds processed this week belong to orders from earlier weeks, so we credit them back.
- "Last week was sent as ₹4,50,000; it's now ₹4,38,000, and the report says so."

## 6. Analyst: send (A5) — Moment 2

- The button reads **Send as incomplete (Instamart missing)**.

Line to say: "The system won't let a report with a hole in it look complete." Click it.

## 7. Founder: the headline (F1)

Switch to **Founder · 10:00**.
- "Consumer sales ₹17.2L, +5% week-on-week."
- Directly under it: "4 of 5 channels · Instamart missing · ₹2,000 unmapped", and the week-on-week compares like with like.

## 8. Founder: the PO trap (F2, F3) — Moment 1

- Blinkit's purchase orders are up 62%, but consumer sales on Blinkit are up 3.8%.
- Turn on **Show what the old Excel would have said**: +21.1% vs the real +5.0%.

Line to say: "This is the mistake the product exists to prevent. Without it, the founder thinks Blinkit is booming and over-orders stock."

## 9. Founder: trust (F6)

Click **I'd act on this**.

Line to say: "That click is our decision-grade trust metric, measured inside the product."

## Optional extras if asked

- **By city:** why Blinkit shows ₹0 in Gurugram (store city vs customer city).
- **Amazon gap:** GST and cancellations explain the whole difference.
- **Reset demo**, then skip the Blinkit upload and send. The headline drops to three channels and says so.
